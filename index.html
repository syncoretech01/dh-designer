01 — Who we are

A Colorado Springs studio for builders, brokers and homeowners who want the room to do the talking.

The Studio

We design homes buyers remember.

DH Designer is a full-service interior design and staging practice based in Colorado Springs. We partner with production builders on model homes, with agents on resale staging, and with families on the kitchens, baths and basements they've been dreaming about.

Every engagement starts with a free model design conversation. We map your goals, walk your floor plan, and return with a concept you can see — palettes, plans and a clear scope. From there we handle sourcing, install and the final styling pass.
